AG Pace privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

AG Pace tracks your group's pace of play during a round of golf. This policy explains what the app collects, what it shares, and how long anything is kept. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

On your phone only

These stay on your phone. They are never sent anywhere by the app.

  • Your location during a round, used to tell when you finish each hole. The app uses GPS only from your tee time until the round ends. Before your tee time it keeps an approximate location session so it can switch GPS on at the right moment; those readings are not used or stored.
  • Round recordings: a log of each round's GPS readings and hole finishes, kept in the app's folder in the Files app so you can review or delete them. The newest 20 are kept.
  • Past rounds: your tee times, hole finish times, and pace for each round you play.
  • Settings: your tee time choices, whether you have seen How it works, and whether sharing is on.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Shared with other AG Pace players: the live map

While your round is on, the app shares your group's position so other AG Pace players on the course can see it on the map. This is on by default and can be turned off at any time with "Share My Position" in Settings, AG Pace.

What is shared, about every 10 seconds while moving and every minute while still:

  • your phone's current position and its accuracy
  • your tee time and starting hole
  • the hole you are on, and whether you are on the tee, playing, or at the green
  • your pace (minutes ahead or behind target)
  • whether your group is riding a cart or walking
  • a random ID created by the app for your phone, so your position replaces itself instead of piling up

Not shared: your name, phone number, email, Apple ID, contacts, or anything else about you. Other players see a dot labeled with your tee time only.

When: only from your tee time until your round ends, and only while sharing is on. Nothing is shared before your tee time, after your round, or when the app is not tracking a round.

How long it is kept: your position is deleted when your round ends. If that does not happen (for example, your phone runs out of battery), other players stop seeing it after 10 minutes without an update, and it is deleted within about an hour. Only your latest position is ever stored; there is no history of where you have been.

Where it is stored: with Supabase, a hosted database service, in its West US (Oregon) region. Supabase stores it on the app's behalf and does not use it for anything else. The random ID is an anonymous sign in that exists only so your phone can update its own position and no one else's.

Photos

When you share a round card, you choose where it goes. Saving it to Photos uses the permission iOS asks for at that moment. The app does not read your photo library.

Children

AG Pace is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the app will show How it works again when what is shared changes.
