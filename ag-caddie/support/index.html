AG Caddie support

Help with AG Caddie, the golf app for Awbrey Glen

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: rodney@rodney.pub. You will usually hear back within a day or two.

In the app, the bug button at the bottom of a screen (or Report a bug or idea in a round's menu) sends a report with a screenshot of that screen, so you do not need to describe where you were.

Common questions

  • Holes are not finishing on their own. AG Caddie needs Location set to Always, with Precise Location on (iPhone Settings, AG Caddie, Location). GPS starts at your tee time, not before.
  • I do not want my group on the course map. Turn off Share My Position in the app's Settings. Yards on the hole map then come from your tee.
  • Do I need an account? No. An account is only needed for Leagues, Challenges and Achievements. Sign up with your name and email, and type the code that is emailed to you.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app's Settings, under your profile, tap Delete. Your account and everything kept with it are deleted. If you no longer have the app, email rodney@rodney.pub from your account's email address and ask for it to be deleted; it is done within a week, and you get a reply when it is.
  • League day notifications. They can be turned off at any time in iPhone Settings, AG Caddie, Notifications. On Android, the round's notification is turned off in Android Settings, Apps, AG Caddie, Notifications.

Privacy

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The privacy policy lists everything the app collects and shares.

AG Caddie is a companion app run by members. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Awbrey Glen Golf Club.
