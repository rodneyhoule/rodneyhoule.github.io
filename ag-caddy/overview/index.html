A golf app for playing Awbrey Glen, built by a member. Green yards, scoring for your group, side games, leagues and a live course map, all in one place.

One app for everyone

Built for iPhone and Android

AG Caddy was written from the start to run on both. The iPhone version goes to the App Store soon, and the Android version is being ported next week, so nobody in your group or your league is left out because of the phone they carry.

It already does everything the app we use for the dogfight does today: scores, handicaps, the games, the results and who owes whom. The rest of this page is what it does better.

Scoring

Scoring that makes sense

  • The score sheet opens by itself when you leave a hole. GPS knows the hole is done, so there's nothing to remember and nothing to tap.
  • Your whole group on one sheet, each player's strokes shown as dots, with net worked out for you.
  • A cleaner card: scores colored against par, stroke dots, and the standings across the top.
  • Marks for each game. In Tilt the card shows each player's multiplier (2x, 3x) for the hole coming up, and the sheet shows the points. In Devil Ball the devil is marked on the card.
Leagues

Leagues where players sign themselves up

  • Members sign up for each league day in the app. We could do away with the Discord poll.
  • A league can be private (members by invite) or public (any member joins with a tap).
  • Admins set the day's games and buy in, make groups and teams, and pick tee times or a shotgun. Everyone gets a notification when the day starts.
  • Anyone in the group can keep the scorecard. The live leaderboard covers every group, and results, payouts and season standings are worked out when the cards are posted.
Course map

See where every group is

  • During a round the map shows every group on the course, labeled only by tee time.
  • When a group is in a blind spot on the hole you're playing, it gets a yellow warning, and the map tells you to hold your shot until they clear.
  • The same map gives you the hole itself: bunkers and water with their yards, a lay up line, a tap for a target, and the slopes on the green.

Getting it

How to get it

AG Caddy isn't on the App Store yet. I'm testing it on my own phone now, the iPhone version goes to the App Store soon, and the Android version follows. Let me know if you'd like it as soon as it's out.

Member accounts (for leagues, challenges and friends) are optional. You sign up with your name and email, and you can delete your account at any time.

Privacy

What is shared

During a round your group's position shows on the map by tee time only, and it's removed when the round ends (you can turn this off). League members see your name, handicap index and league day scores. Everything else stays on your phone. The full list is in the privacy policy.

AG Caddy is a companion app run by members. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Awbrey Glen Golf Club.
