The Men's Majors

Five tournaments make up the major season. Playing in them earns Awbrey Cup points.

1Flat vs. RoundTwo days in June, Friday and Saturday▸

Season opener3 pts

The most social event on the calendar. The field splits into two teams by age: the Flat Bellies and the Round Bellies. There is no fixed age cutoff. It is set each year by the average age of everyone who signs up, so the dividing line moves with the field.

  • Each side has a captain. The night before, a pairings party is held where the captains set the matchups.
  • Format: two-person best ball over two days.
  • Points are awarded for the front nine, the back nine, and the overall eighteen, so three points are available in every match.

New and want to meet people? This is the one. It puts different generations of members in the same groups, and the pairings party will do more to get you connected than anything else we run.

2Men's Member GuestThree days in July, Thursday through Saturday, 60 teams▸

Lottery entry3 pts

The club's premiere event. You invite a guest from outside the club and play as a two-person team.

Registration is a lottery, not first come, first served.

  • The entry window opens for five days in early March, from 7:00 am Monday to 5:00 pm Friday.
  • When you enter during that window makes no difference. Early and late entries have identical odds. There is no advantage to rushing.
  • Sports memberships and members who have submitted a resignation are not eligible.
  • The draw happens that Saturday morning using randomized lottery software, which also ranks the remaining teams into a waitlist.
  • Teams drawn into the field are charged a non-refundable deposit to hold the spot. It applies toward the entry fee.
  • After the field and waitlist are set, registration reopens to the remaining membership levels.

Thursday is not a warm up day. It includes a practice round shotgun, a best ball tournament, multi-staged putting contests, and The Gathering, all with real payouts. Friday and Saturday are flighted, with a horserace for the flight winners. Entry includes a tee prize.

Premium event with the highest entry fee of the season. Check the calendar for current pricing.

3Men's Club ChampionshipThree days in August, Friday through Sunday▸

Three divisions3 pts

Club champions are determined by gross scores. Net payouts are also provided.

Which division am I in?
Enter your age to see your division and tees.

Championship / Open Division, under 55, Black Tees. Thirty-six holes of stroke play qualifying, then match play for the title.

  • Friday: Qualifying Round 1, tee times from the early afternoon
  • Saturday: Qualifying Round 2, morning start
  • Sunday: the top four qualifiers enter match play to determine the Club Champion. Everyone else plays a third round of stroke play to decide the Open Division net winners.
  • Payouts: the top four from the match play bracket, plus first through third net across the three stroke play rounds.

Senior Division, ages 55 to 69, Gold/White Tees. Thirty-six holes of stroke play on Saturday and Sunday only. Payouts: Senior Club Champion, second and third gross, and first through third net.

Super Senior Division, 70 and up, White Tees. Thirty-six holes of stroke play on Saturday and Sunday, the same weekend as the other divisions. Payouts: Super Senior Club Champion, second and third gross, and first through third net.

Rule of 80. Available in the Senior and Super Senior divisions for net competition only. If you elect to use it, you are no longer eligible for the gross competition.

All start times are estimates. Final times depend on the size of the field and your position in it. Entry includes elevated payouts, trophies for the champions, and a hosted meal after the final round.

4Men's Member MemberTwo days in September, Friday and Saturday▸

Opens about 5 weeks out, 7:00 am3 pts

One of the most popular events of the year. Teams of two members, with a maximum course handicap spread of 8 between partners. The field splits into four flights by combined handicap index, and the top teams in each flight qualify for the Championship Horserace.

You play four nine-hole rounds, two each day, returning to the clubhouse after each one. Each round is a different format: Net Better Ball, Chapman, Scramble, and Alternate Shot. Handicap allowances differ by format and are posted with the event.

Rule of 80 applies here as well and moves you up one set of tees:

Flights 3 and 4WhiteGreen

Entry includes Champions trophies, payouts, and meals after both rounds.

Spots go fast. Have your partner committed before registration opens.

5Awbrey CupTwo days in early October, invitation only, no entry fee▸

Season finaleCannot sign up

The only event you cannot sign up for. The field is the top 24 point earners of the season. It is fully funded by the Men's Golf Association, so there is no cost to play.

Two captains are selected, the two teams are drafted, and pairings are set for two days of competition. Standings are published throughout the season, so you can see where you sit at any point.

+Men's Spring TripUsually in May▸

An away trip organized each spring, open to the membership. Destination, format, and dates change from year to year, and details are announced on the member calendar.

Weekly games

Filter by how you like to play.

1 pt per week

Wednesday Sweeps

$10  Wednesdays at 1:00 pm

The main weekly men's game. Usually a shotgun start, occasionally tee times. The format rotates every week, so you might play a scramble one week, a shamble the next, then one gross and one net. Pairings are typically ABCD, which groups you across handicap levels so you play with different people constantly. Closest to the pin every week. Payouts are in shop credit.

Optional dinner after the round on the first Wednesday of each month (opt in when you register). The season opens with a Kick Off Dinner in April and closes with the Men's Closer in October.

2 pts per playEasiest to join

Saturday Morning Game

$10  Saturdays, tee time before 1:00 pm

A two-person partner game with a rotating format each week, plus rotating skins that alternate between gross and net. Payouts are in shop credit.

No advance sign up and no partner required. Book a Saturday tee time before 1:00 pm, tell the golf shop at check in that you want in, and they will pair you with someone if you are on your own. Points at double the rate of Sweeps.

Friday Skins

$10 cash  Fridays, tee times from 11:00 am

Your $10 buys you into both the gross and net skins pots. Sign up on the member calendar. Settle up in cash in the restaurant after the round.

9 After 5

$5 cash  Wednesdays at 5:30 pm, shotgun

Nine holes in the evening with a rotating format. Cash buy in, cash payouts, settled in the restaurant afterward. Sign up on the member calendar.

Designed for younger working members, with a start late enough to play after a workday. Bring your spouse or any partner you like, and play from whatever tee suits you.

Friday Skins and 9 After 5 are organized by members rather than the golf shop, and do not earn points.

How to sign up

Almost everything is booked through the member calendar on the Awbrey Glen website. You will need to be logged in to the member site to see it. Events fill long before they are played, so know when registration opens.
